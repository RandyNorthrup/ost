Summarize the meeting in **Markdown format** using a structured two-part layout:
1. A fluent **Overview Summary**
2. A set of **Focus Area Updates**

## GOAL
This task is an **executive-level summary**. It must capture the **purpose, major topics, and key outcomes** of the meeting in a form that is clear, actionable, and suitable for leaders who did **not attend**.
The output must be **concise**, **standalone**, and **results-focused** — not a transcript, but a high-signal summary.
 
---
 
## Section Definitions
 
### 1. Overview
 
#### Purpose
Provide a **high-level, fluent overview** of the meeting’s purpose and major themes. This should help an executive understand **why the meeting took place** and **what it generally covered**, without diving into specific details.
 
#### Format
- Write a **single paragraph** of up to **4 well-connected sentences**
- Do **not** use lists, bullets, or itemized phrasing
- The tone should be **natural, cohesive, and executive-facing**
 
#### Content Guidelines
- Focus on **broad subject areas** or themes, not individual discussion points
- Describe the **overall purpose** of the meeting
- Summarize the **main topics or goals** that were addressed
 
#### What to Exclude
- Do **not** include names of participants
- Do **not** mention specific proposals, outcomes, or follow-ups
- Do **not** list topics or use fragmented sentence structure
 
### 2. Key updates
 
#### Purpose
Provide a **rich yet to-the-point summary** of each key discussion topic. Each bullet should contain enough context and detail so that an executive can understand the important points and next steps **without needing to revisit the recording**.
 
#### Format
- Write a bullet list (-) of **Key Area Updates**, each representing a **single discussion topic**
- Each bullet must:
  - Start with a **short, bolded title** naming the topic
  - Be written as **1–2 standalone sentences**
  - Be **self-contained**, clear, and follow the **chronological order** of the meeting
 
#### Required Content per Bullet
Each Key Area Update must include the following four elements:
1. **Subject** — what was discussed
2. **Speaker(s)** — who contributed (first names only, if relevant)
3. **Insight or Update** — what was said, proposed, or explained
4. **Outcome or Next Step** — what was concluded, decided, or planned
 
#### Include Specific Details When Available
To make the content more actionable and avoid ambiguity, include:
- Key **numbers**, **metrics**, **dates**, or **deadlines**
- **Quantitative** or **factual** data that supports the update
- Any **agreed frameworks**, **formats**, or **definitions**
---
 
## CLARITY AND FOCUS
- Each section must be free from filler, vague language, or conversational tone.
- The output must present only what is **important, actionable, and clearly communicated**.
- Bullets must reflect **real content** — not assumptions or speculation.

- Do **not** include paragraph-length bullets
- Do **not** reference shared screens, slides, or visuals
- Do **not** include any conversational text, introductions (e.g., 'Here is the summary'), or concluding questions.
