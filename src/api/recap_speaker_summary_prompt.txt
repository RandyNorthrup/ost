### Detailed Speaker-Based Meeting Recap (Markdown Format)

**Goal**
Produce a **Markdown-formatted recap** of the meeting, structured **by speaker** rather than by topic. The recap must ensure **full contextualization** and **complete coverage** of the meeting content, capturing all discussions, decisions, and perspectives in a speaker-organized format. This structure is intended to help readers **understand each participant’s contribution in detail**, especially if they did not attend the meeting.
 
### **Rules**
 
#### **1. Structure**
- Organize the output as a **list of speaker components**, where **each component corresponds to a speaker who spoke during the meeting**.
- The component title must be the **first name** of the speaker. Use the **full name** only if multiple people share the same first name (based on {$MEETING_SPEAKERS$}).
- **Do not sort speakers alphabetically.** Instead, order them **by length of their component**, starting with the speaker who contributed the most.
- Each speaker’s component should contain a **series of detailed explanations**, mirroring the level of detail in the original topic-based format.
 
##### Additional Requirements for Each Speaker’s Component:
- Begin each explanation with a **short and representative, bolded title** summarizing the main point, followed by a **thorough paragraph** elaborating on the details.
- Include the following in each explanation, where applicable:
  - **Context**, reasoning, or **background** for the discussion or decision.
  - Differing perspectives or detailed **contributions from other participants**.
  - Decisions made and their **implications or consequences**.
  - Specific examples, **metrics**, technical explanations, workflows, or analogies provided to support understanding.
- **All explanations under each speaker must be fully self-contained, detailed, and descriptive**, with no vague summaries or single-sentence explanations.
 
#### **2. Speaker References**
- Always refer to the **component speaker by their first name**.
- For other speakers mentioned:
  - Use their **first name**, or their **pronouns** **only if** these are provided in {$MEETING_SPEAKERS$}.
  - If pronouns are not available, **default to first name only**.
- Do **not** refer to greetings, welcome messages, nudges, meeting setup, or thanks — these are not useful for recap purposes.
 
#### **3. Clarity and Accessibility**
- Briefly **explain technical terms, acronyms, or workflows** for unfamiliar readers.
- Avoid vague descriptions like “X and Y discussed.” Instead:
  - Include **what exactly was discussed**, **why**, **by whom**, and **with what conclusion or follow-up**.
 
#### **4. Balance Detail and Brevity**
- Include **comprehensive and essential information** without redundancy.
- Use clear **examples, analogies, or illustrations** to simplify complex concepts.
- **Ensure every explanation is complete and rich in information.**

#### **5. Output Format**
- Translate content into {$LANGUAGE_INSTRUCTION$}, 
- Do NOT ask for confirmation or permission to proceed.
- Do NOT include any conversational text, introductions (e.g., 'Here is the summary', 'Below is your'), or concluding questions.
- Output only the Markdown content starting with the first speaker header.

### **Evaluation Metrics**
 
#### **Coverage**
- How well the recap captures all relevant content from all participants during the meeting.
 
#### **Level of Details**
- How comprehensively the details discussed during a meeting are reflected in the **Detailed Explanations**.
