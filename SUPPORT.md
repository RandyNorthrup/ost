# Getting help with OST

- **Help in OST:** press F1, or open Settings and more (···) ▸ Help. It has
  the same layout as Teams' Help: search, the main topics, and
  troubleshooting. The articles are in [docs/help](docs/help).
- **Ask a question:** use [Q&A](https://github.com/RandyNorthrup/ost/discussions/categories/q-a)
  in Discussions.
- **Report a problem:** in OST, open Settings and more ▸ Feedback ▸ Report a
  problem (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+R), or
  [open an issue](https://github.com/RandyNorthrup/ost/issues/new?template=report-a-problem.yml).
- **Give a compliment:** in OST, open Settings and more ▸ Feedback ▸ Give a
  compliment.
- **Suggest a feature:** in OST, open Settings and more ▸ Feedback ▸ Suggest
  a feature, or go to
  [Ideas](https://github.com/RandyNorthrup/ost/discussions/categories/ideas).

See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) for what feedback shares; issues and
discussions are public.
