# Teams' calendar — spec (TASK-0050)

Measured in Teams on the web as ost-test1 on 2026-11-17, inside OST's own
web profile on linuxlt (1852×988 viewport, 1x). Teams' calendar is Outlook
on the web's calendar hosted in an iframe
(`outlook.office.com/hosted/calendar/…`); its scheduling form and event
page for Teams meetings ("calv2") are Teams' own. The same app runs in
Teams desktop.

## Requests

| What | Request |
| --- | --- |
| Week data | Outlook's `outlookgatewayb2/graphql` and `service.svc?action=GetCalendarView`; OST reads Graph `/me/calendarView` with `Prefer: IdType="ImmutableId"` (the ids Outlook uses). |
| RSVP (card, event page, "RSVP with note") | `POST outlook.office.com/owa/service.svc?action=RespondToCalendarEvent&app=Calendar`, empty body; the JSON in the `x-owa-urlpostdata` header: `RespondToCalendarEventJsonRequest` with `EventId` (the occurrence, or the series), `Response` Accept/Decline/Tentative, `SendResponse` ("Email organizer"), `Notes` `<div>note</div>` (HTML), `Attendance` 0, `Mode` 0; headers `action`, `x-req-source: Calendar`, `prefer: IdType="ImmutableId", exchange.behavior="IncludeThirdPartyOnlineMeetingProviders"`.  Follow is `Response: "Tentative"` with `Attendance: 3` (Teams desktop bundle: AttendanceType None 0, InPerson 1, Online 2, Follow 3); Outlook then marks the event free and its subject "Following: ". The token is the hosted calendar's (app ceb96695-e468-48ba-ba21-35e2a242d396 through Teams' broker, scope outlook.office.com/.default); the service also needs a browser User-Agent, `x-owa-hosted-ux: true`, `x-owa-host-app: teams_desktop` and the JSON encoded as encodeURIComponent (a "+" for a space fails with TimeZoneException). |
| RSVP from Teams' own event page (calv2) | `PUT {mt}/v2.0/me/calendars/events/{iCalUId}/respond?eventType=Single|Occurrence|RecurringMaster&eventStartDateTime=…` `{responseType: Accept|TentativelyAccept|Decline, sendResponse, comment, attendance: None|Follow}` (Teams' calendar service bundle). OST uses it when the sign-in is device code (no broker). |
| Send update / Cancel (organizer) | `PUT {mt}/v2.0/me/calendars/events/{objectId}?isOnlineMeeting=…` `{updatedCalendarEvent}`; `PUT {mt}/v2.0/me/calendars/events/{objectId}/cancel` `{cancellationMessage}` (objectId: the REST id). |
| Form opens | `PUT {mt}/v2.0/me/calendars/schedules` (free/busy, "AttendeeAvailabilityOnly"; answers each person's events and the organizer's time zone, e.g. `PacificSt`), `POST {mt}/beta/me/calendarEvents/privateMeeting/schedulingService/create` `{"isStreamEnabled":false,"meetingType":"Scheduled"}` (the meeting's thread, links, dial-in). |
| Send / Save | `POST {mt}/v2.0/me/calendars/default/events?isOnlineMeeting=true&shouldDecryptData=true` with `{calendarEvent, schedulingServiceEvent}`; the event: attendees (organizer first, `role` Admin, `type` Organizer/Required/Optional), `bodyContent`, `startTime`/`endTime` with offset, `eventTimeZone` (PacificSt), `eventType` Single/RecurringMaster, `eventRecurrencePattern` `{patternType (Daily 1, Weekly 2, Monthly 3, RelativeMonthly 4, Yearly 5…), daily/weekly/monthly/yearly: {interval, daysOfTheWeek, dayOfMonth…}}`, `eventRecurrenceRange` `{startDate, endDate}`, `isAppointment` (no attendees), `location`, `showAs`, `reminderMinutesBeforeStart` 15, `skypeTeamsData`. Without the scheduling event the middle tier answers 400 "Scheduling service event cannot be null for online meeting". |
| Channel meeting | `POST {mt}/v2.0/groups/smtp:<team mailbox>/calendars/default/events?isOnlineMeeting=true&shouldDecryptData=false`, the event alone, the team's mailbox as a Required attendee, `mailboxAddress`, `skypeTeamsData` `{"cid":"19:…@thread.tacv2","uid":"8:orgid:…","type":0}`; the service posts the meeting in the channel. |
| Cancel a channel meeting | `PUT {mt}/v2.0/groups/smtp:<mailbox>/calendars/events/<iCalUId>/cancel` `{conversationId, messageId, rootMessageId, cancellationMessageFormat:"The meeting \"{0}\" is cancelled", fetchEventContract:{eventType, eventTimeZone:"UTC", startTime}}`. |
| Meet now | `POST {mt}/beta/me/calendarEvents/privateMeeting/schedulingService/create` `{"meetingType":"MeetNow","isStreamEnabled":false,"subject":"Meeting with OST Test 1","unhideChatThread":true}`, then pre-join on its join link. |
| Join with an ID | `GET {mt}/beta/meetings/identifiers?meetingId=…&passcode=…&link=https://teams.cloud.microsoft/meet/<id>?p=<passcode>` (answers the tenant), then the pre-join's conversation with `meetingData {meetingCode, passcode, meetingUrl}` in place of the thread (`groupChat` and `meetingInfo` null, joinType MeetingData). Its answer names the meeting: thread, organizer, tenant, subject. OST joins that conversation, reads them, leaves, and joins by link. |
| Meeting start notification and join bar | The call manager's evt 128 `MeetingStart` (Meet now is `HuddleMeetingStart`; `MeetingStartFromLobby` is dropped) with `threadId, callId, subject, messageId, rsvp, meetingJoinUrl`. Shown when Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ "Meeting start notifications" (toastSettings.MeetingWentLiveNotification) is on, you didn't start it, aren't in it, the chat isn't muted, and the meeting is in your calendar from 15 minutes before its start to 5 after its end (not declined). The banner: the subject, "{{meetingCaller}} started the meeting", Join and Chat. The join bar (LiveMeetingStatusBar, "Show meeting join bar", toastSettings.liveMeetingStatusBanner): "Meeting Started: {{meetingTitle}}" / "Meetings Started: {{meetingTitle}} +{{meetingsCount}}", Join, "View meetings" (each meeting), "View in Calendar"; it drops a meeting when you join it. Measured in Teams desktop on winpc (2026-11-17): 36 tall over the whole window under the title bar, colorBrandBackground2 (#E8EBFA) with a 0.67 colorBrandStroke2 (#C5CBFA) bottom edge; Video 20 filled at x 12 and the text at x 40, 14/20 in colorBrandForeground1 (#4F52B2); one action, 12 right of it the 24 x 23.33 Dismiss and 12 to the edge: for one meeting Join (primary small, 64 x 23.33, 12/400), for several "View meetings" (secondary, #D1D1D1 edge), which opens the "Live meetings" dialog ("View in Calendar" is that button's text only while Teams' multi-meeting dialog flag is off). The dialog: 600 wide, padding 24, title 20/28 semibold, Dismiss 32 x 31.33; a card per meeting (550.67 x 139.33, 8 apart; white radius 8 with the depth-2 shadow round a brand fill, radius 4, padding 16): the show-as bar (4 x 39; hatched #E8EBFA/brand at 48° while unanswered), the start time 14/24 semibold lower case and its length 12/16 ("1 hr") in a 90 column at x 36; at x 126 the subject 14/20 semibold, the location and "{{organizer}} invited you" (not when you organized it) 12/16; right, Chat (secondary, Chat 20 glyph, 66.33 x 23.33) and Join (Video 16, 65.33 x 24), 8 apart, 16 from the edge; 78 below, how long the call has run ("05:49") at x 43 and the people in it (28 px avatars) at x 141. Chat opens the meeting chat, Join its pre-join; both close the dialog. |

- Hours 80 px; hour lines #D1D1D1, half-hour lines dashed; labels 11 px at x 10.
- Events: y = start + 2, height = duration − 5; radius 4; fill rgba(199,201,238,.9) busy, rgba(225,225,246,.9) free/tentative/unanswered; 4 px left bar: busy solid brand, tentative or not answered hatched, free white with a brand edge; title 12/600 and the location/organizer 12/400 at x 12, y 4; a 30-minute event shows them on one line. A series' occurrence has the repeat icon (an exception the broken one) bottom right. From five minutes before the start a Teams meeting's tile has Join ("Join Teams meeting"), 4.4 from the right and 5.8 down: brand with a white glyph while its call is going on, else white with a #BDBDBD edge and a #616161 glyph; beside other events (lanes) only the 20 x 20 Video glyph (padding 0 4, radius 4), alone the "Join" label (Teams desktop, winpc, 2026-11-17).
- Overlaps: lanes, earlier first and then shorter first; lane width (column − 14.6 − 4·(n−1))/n, 2.6 in, 4 apart.
- Column widths (Teams desktop on winpc, 2026-11-17, swept from 1232 to 2432 wide): the calendar (the hosted Outlook frame, everything right of the app rail, side pane included) lays out wide above 1550 px, whether or not the side pane shows. Narrow: every day the same width, lanes or not, and short day names ("Mon"). Wide: full day names ("Monday"), and in the week holding today, with u = grid width / days, today is 1.5·u and tomorrow (if shown) 1.3·u; the other days share the rest, a past day two thirds of a later one (weights 2 and 3). Lanes never widen a day. Weeks without today: equal. (Work week at 1832: Mon 168.4, Tue 459.1, Wed 398, Thu and Fri 252.6; Week: Sun and Mon 141.3, Tue 328, Wed 284.3, Thu–Sat 211.9.)
- Backgrounds: past days and hours outside 8–17 #FAFAFA; a day under an all-day busy event #EEEFF9.
- An event crossing midnight is drawn in both days; one spanning a full day or starting/ending at midnight goes to the strip (Teams' isMultipleFullDays).

## Event page and form

Teams' calv2 page: header (32 px brand tile, title 18/700, tabs Chat · Details · Scheduling Assistant · …; Join/Send and Close 96×32); a command bar (Cancel meeting, Copy link, Show as ▾ …; an invitation: Accept, Decline, Tentative, RSVP with note); the form (fields 1037 wide from x 74: title, attendees with "+ Optional", Facilitator, date/time pickers with the duration and All day, repeat, Add channel, location with Online meeting, the details editor and its toolbar, Add an agenda) and a right pane (Options; Tracking on an existing meeting). See MeetingForm.qml for every measured size.

- Repeat: Does not repeat, Every weekday (Mon - Fri), Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly, Custom; anything but the first opens "Set recurrence" (Start, Repeat every N unit, weekday circles, End with Remove, a summary such as "Occurs every Monday through Friday until 11/19/2026"), whose summary then labels the dropdown.
- With a channel: Send (not Save), Category disabled, no Facilitator or Online meeting toggle, "OST Test Team > OST test channel" with "Send personal invites".
- "RSVP to <title> with note": 600 wide, "Add a note", a response dropdown (Accept, Decline, Tentative), Confirm / Cancel. Teams on the web offers no Follow; OST sends "Follow" as Outlook's response for it.
- "Cancel meeting?": a cancellation note (optional) and "Cancel meeting".
- "Start a meeting now": Meeting name ("Meeting with <you>"), Start meeting, Get a link to share.
- "Join a meeting with an ID": Meeting ID (ⓘ "You'll find the meeting ID with the other join info near the bottom of the meeting invite."), Meeting passcode, "Join Meeting" (disabled until both are typed).
