# Manage notifications

Open **Settings ▸ Notifications and activity**:

- Choose what notifies you for chats, mentions, replies, reactions and
  more: **Show in Activity and banner**, **Show in Activity**, or **Off**.
- Turn sounds on or off, and choose whether notifications show while
  you're in calls and meetings.
- **Test notification** shows what one looks like.

For one chat, use its **···** menu: **Mute** or **Turn off notifications**.
For a channel, use **··· ▸ Manage notifications**.
