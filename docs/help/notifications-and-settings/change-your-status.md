# Change your status

1. Select your picture in the top-right corner.
2. Select your current status, then pick **Available**, **Busy**, **Do not
   disturb**, **Be right back**, **Appear away** or **Appear offline**.
   Choose **Reset status** to let OST set it from your activity.
3. **Set status message** adds a note that people see when they look at
   you or message you.

You can also change your status from OST's icon in the system tray.
