# Hold, transfer or consult

In a call, open **More (···)**:

- **Hold** puts the other person on hold (they hear hold music). Select
  **Resume** to come back.
- **Transfer** hands the call to someone you pick.
- **Consult then transfer** puts the caller on hold and opens a chat with
  the person first. Then choose **Transfer** or **Resume**.
- **Dial pad** sends tones (for menus that ask you to press a number).
