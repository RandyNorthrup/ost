# Use OneDrive in OST

Select **OneDrive** in the app bar:

- **Home** has your recent files, **My files** your folders, and **Shared**
  what others shared with you.
- **Favorites**, **Libraries** (your teams' document libraries) and the
  **Recycle bin** are on the left.
- Open a file in the browser, download it, upload files, make folders and
  delete them.
