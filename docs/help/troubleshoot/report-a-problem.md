# Report a problem

1. Select **Settings and more (···) ▸ Feedback ▸ Report a problem**, or
   press **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+R**.
2. Say what happened. Add a screenshot (**Capture screenshot**) or files
   if they help.
3. Choose whether to share OST's recent log lines, and whether we may
   contact you.
4. Select **Submit**. OST opens an issue on OST's GitHub in your browser
   with only what you typed, and opens the report's folder on your device.
   It holds **OST report details.txt** (OST's version, your system, where
   you sent it from and, if you chose to share them, OST's recent log
   lines) and your screenshots.
5. Review the details file, then drop it and any screenshots you want to
   share into the issue (your first screenshot is already on the
   clipboard: Ctrl+V), and select **Create**.

Nothing leaves your device until you select **Create**. Reports are
**public**. Names, addresses, message text and tokens are masked in the
log lines. See the [privacy statement](https://github.com/RandyNorthrup/ost/blob/main/PRIVACY.md#feedback).

**Give a compliment** works the same way. **Suggest a feature** opens the
project's Ideas, where you can vote on what others suggest.
