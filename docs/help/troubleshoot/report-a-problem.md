# Report a problem

1. Select **Settings and more (···) ▸ Feedback ▸ Report a problem**, or
   press **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+R**.
2. Say what happened. Add a screenshot (**Capture screenshot**) or files
   if they help.
3. Choose whether to share OST's recent log lines, and whether we may
   contact you.
4. Select **Submit**. OST shows what GitHub will receive: a title made
   from your text's first line, your text, and your answer about being
   contacted. Then choose one:
   - **Open filled-in form** opens the issue on OST's GitHub in your
     browser with those filled in. GitHub receives them as soon as the page
     opens, even if you never select **Create**.
   - **Copy text and open a blank form** puts your text on the clipboard
     and opens an empty issue form. GitHub gets nothing you typed until you
     paste it in (Ctrl+V) and select **Create**.
   - **Back** returns to the form.

   OST also opens the report's folder on your device. It holds **OST
   report details.txt** (OST's version, your system, where you sent it
   from and, if you chose to share them, OST's recent log lines) and your
   screenshots.
5. Review the details file, then drop it and any screenshots you want to
   share into the issue, and select **Create**. With the filled-in form,
   your first screenshot is already on the clipboard (Ctrl+V).

The details file, log lines and screenshots stay on your device unless you
attach them. Reports are **public**. Names, addresses, message text and
tokens are masked in the log lines. See the
[privacy statement](https://github.com/RandyNorthrup/ost/blob/main/PRIVACY.md#feedback).

**Give a compliment** works the same way. **Suggest a feature** opens the
project's Ideas, where you can vote on what others suggest.
