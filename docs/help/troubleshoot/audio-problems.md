# Fix audio and video problems

- Check **Settings ▸ Devices**: the right microphone, speaker and camera,
  and that the test works.
- In a call, **More (···) ▸ Settings ▸ Audio settings** switches devices.
- Check that you're not muted (the **Mic** button), and that your system's
  sound settings aren't muting OST.
- Sharing your screen needs your desktop's screen-sharing portal
  (xdg-desktop-portal with your desktop's backend).
- Still stuck? Right-click OST's icon in the system tray and select
  **Help ▸ Collect Support Files**, then **Report a problem**.
