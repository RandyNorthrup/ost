# Hide or leave a chat

In the chat list, point at a chat and select **···**:

- **Hide** takes the chat out of the list until someone sends something
  new. **Unhide** brings it back.
- **Leave** (group chats) takes you out of the chat. The others see that
  you left.
- **Mute** stops its notifications; **Turn off notifications** does the
  same for a chat's activity.
- **Mark as read** and **Mark as unread** change its unread state.
- **Move to section** files it under Favorites or a section of your own.
