# Use threads in a channel

In a channel that uses threads, messages show as a chat.

- **Reply in thread** (in a message's menu) opens the thread on the right.
- **Open threads** at the top lists every thread, newest reply first.
- In a thread, **Send to: Thread only ▾** can also send your reply to the
  channel (**Thread and channel**).
- **Follow thread** gets you notified of new replies.
