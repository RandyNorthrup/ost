# Share your screen

1. In the call or meeting toolbar, select **Share**.
2. Your desktop's picker asks what to share. Pick a screen or a window.
3. OST shows **You're presenting**, and Share becomes **Stop sharing**.

To stop, select **Stop sharing**, or close the window you're sharing.
When someone else shares, their screen takes the stage.
