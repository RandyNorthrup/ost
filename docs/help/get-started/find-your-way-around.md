# Find your way around OST

OST is laid out like Teams:

- **The app bar** on the left: Activity, Chat, Calendar, Calls and
  OneDrive. **View more apps (···)** holds more apps, such as Planner,
  OneNote and People.
- **The list** next to it. In Chat, it lists your chats, teams and channels,
  with the filters Unread, Channels and Chats.
- **The conversation** on the right, with its tabs and the message box.
- **Search** at the top (Ctrl+E) finds people, chats and channels.
- **Settings and more (···)**, next to your picture, has Settings,
  Help (F1), Feedback and Keyboard shortcuts.
- **Your picture** sets your status and status message.
